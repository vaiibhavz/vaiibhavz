## About Me
I'm a UI/UX Designer and Computer Science student focused on building practical, meaningful, and user-centric digital experiences. By bridging the gap between technical engineering and human-centered design, I aim to create intuitive interfaces for complex systems. 

I’m particularly drawn to simplicity, strong visual systems, and solving problems with thoughtful design rather than decorative chaos.

**I enjoy:**
- Designing intuitive interfaces and exploring product thinking
- The intersection of behavioral psychology, AI, and user experience
- Learning design systems and multimodal interactions
- Improving with every iteration
